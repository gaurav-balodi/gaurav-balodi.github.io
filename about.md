# About Gaurav Balodi

I am a Senior Software Engineer with more than 12 years of experience building, stabilizing, and scaling web applications, with a strong focus on Ruby on Rails and backend engineering.

My experience spans SaaS, FinTech, Healthcare, EdTech, and restaurant technology. I have worked on microservices, RESTful APIs, POS and third-party integrations, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, performance optimization, and end-to-end product development.

## What I bring

- **Backend and architecture:** Ruby on Rails, service integrations, distributed systems, API design, and scalable application architecture.
- **Production excellence:** diagnosing bottlenecks, improving response times, strengthening observability, and addressing reliability and security concerns.
- **Engineering quality:** automated testing, code reviews, refactoring, maintainability, and collaborative delivery.
- **Cloud and operations:** AWS services, Docker, CI/CD, and application monitoring.

I enjoy balancing feature delivery with the work that keeps production systems stable, understandable, and maintainable.
